Midnight Mews

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

This policy covers the Midnight Mews app on iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play), an app of narrated sleep stories about cats. It is published by Hosein Abedi, who is responsible for the information described here. You can reach him at midnightmewssupport@gmail.com.

The short version: there are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. What you tell the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

These are kept only on your device. We never receive them, and deleting the app deletes them.

  • Your name, if you choose to give one, so the app can greet you.
  • The date you first opened the app. It lets the app recognise early listeners if paid options are offered later.
  • Stories you save for offline listening, and the list of which ones you saved.
  • A copy of the story list, so the app can open without a connection.
  • The last story you played, and how far into it you got, so you can pick it up again.
  • Small preferences, such as whether you have already seen the player's hints.

On Android, the app is excluded from Google's device backup, so none of this leaves the phone. On iPhone, if you back up your phone to iCloud or a computer, Apple may include these items in your own backup. That backup belongs to you, and we have no access to it.

What the app requests from the internet

To show the cats and play their stories, the app downloads the story list and the pictures and audio from our servers. We use two providers for this: Supabase, for the story list, and Cloudflare, for pictures and audio. Like any web server, they receive your device's IP address and basic technical details of each request, such as the device type and the time. We don't use this to identify you, and nothing you have told the app is sent with these requests. All of these connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail).

Apple and Google

Downloads, updates and ratings go through the App Store or Google Play, under Apple's or Google's own privacy policies. If you have allowed your phone to share crash reports and usage data with app developers, Apple or Google may pass us crash reports and overall statistics, such as the number of installs. These don't include your name or contact details, and we use them only to fix problems.

Who receives information

We share information only with the kinds of service providers named above, and only so the app and support can work:

  • Hosting providers (Supabase and Cloudflare), which deliver the story list, pictures and audio.
  • Our email provider (Google), if you write to us.
  • The app stores (Apple and Google), as described above.

We use only providers whose terms require them to protect this information and to use it only to provide their service. We don't sell information, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or let anyone use it for their own purposes. We would disclose it to authorities only if the law required us to.

How long information is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • Server logs: we don't download or keep them. Supabase and Cloudflare hold them for a short period under their standard log retention, then delete them automatically.
  • Emails: as long as needed to answer you and keep track of the conversation. We delete them sooner if you ask.
  • Crash reports and statistics from the app stores: for as long as Apple or Google keep them in our developer accounts.

Security

The app stores no personal information on our servers. Everything it downloads travels over encrypted connections, the servers hold only the stories themselves, and access to our provider accounts is limited to the publisher. No system is perfectly secure, but keeping almost everything on your phone means there is very little that could be exposed.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share information with anyone for their own purposes.
  • No access to your microphone, location, contacts, photos or health data.
  • No sleep tracking.

Children

Midnight Mews does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no accounts, no ads and no way to contact other people.

Your rights and choices

Because your name and saved stories stay on your device, you can remove a saved story from its player screen, or remove everything, your name included, by deleting the app. Wherever you live, you can ask us what information about you we hold, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, including any emails you have sent us. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, these are also your legal rights. We process the limited technical information above on the basis of our legitimate interest in delivering the app and keeping it working. Write to midnightmewssupport@gmail.com with any request. You may also complain to your local data protection authority; in the Netherlands, that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Our providers may process data outside the European Union. Where they do, they rely on the safeguards the law requires, such as standard contractual clauses.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date. If an update to the app would collect anything not described here, this page will be updated before that version is released.
