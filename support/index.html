Midnight Mews

Narrated stories for falling asleep. Pick a cat, pick one of that cat's nights, and let a quiet voice tell it.

Support

For questions, problems, or anything that kept you awake, write to midnightmewssupport@gmail.com.

Can I listen without a connection?

Yes, once a story is saved. Open the story and tap Save offline. A saved story plays with no connection. The app needs a connection the first time it opens, to fetch the cats.

Why can't I open some of the cats?

Their stories are still being recorded. They show Coming soon until they are ready, and open on their own once they are, with no update needed.

Will the story stop on its own?

Use the sleep timer on the player. It fades the story out slowly over about twenty seconds, then pauses. It keeps working with the screen locked.

Where is my name kept?

Only on your phone. See the privacy policy.
